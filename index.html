Every tracked stock's % move from its previous close fans out of one point at the open into the gate: bulls collect at the top, bears at the bottom. Energy flows into the 3 strongest on each side and on into the cards, strongest on the right. The white line is the selected instrument from 1-minute bars, refreshed every 5 s. Tags show order-block status (no open-interest data on this feed). Indicative Yahoo data, not an exchange tape.

Order blocks: last opposite candle before a ≥1.5×ATR move on today's 5-minute bars, still unmitigated. Stops sit beyond the zone; targets are swing levels or R-multiples when no swing exists. Educational only, not advice.

Fresh order blocks today

Every fresh order block formed since the 09:15 IST open (a move of at least 1.5×ATR, still unmitigated), newest first. Weak zones are left out. Educational only, not advice.

Market predictor

Odds for the next daily candle from a machine-learning ensemble (logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting) trained on 2 years of daily candles from every tracked instrument. It reads recent returns, the last 3 candles, the close within the day's range, stretch from the 20-day average, range, gap, volume and RSI. Each arrow's thickness and percentage is the probability of that move, measured from the last close in ATRs (average daily range). Stocks are ranked by their most likely move (up or down; ★ marks it). Daily moves are mostly noise, so expect odds well below certainty. Educational only, not advice.
